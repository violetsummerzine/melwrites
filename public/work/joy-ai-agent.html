Joy AI Agent

Joy AI Agent is an AI reading companion that makes a single Web3 article accessible to four audiences. Same content, infinitely personalized. Winner of a Vercel hackathon in Miami, 2026.

Overview

What it is

An AI reading companion that makes a single Web3 article accessible to four audiences, adapting complexity, tone, and context based on whether you’re a fashion enthusiast, entrepreneur, developer, or investor.

  • Fashion enthusiast
  • Entrepreneur
  • Developer
  • Investor

Same content, infinitely personalized.

Build

How I built it

I used v0, Vercel’s coding agent, to integrate the AI reading companion into Violet Verse. The companion reads the content on the page and generates a response based on the reader’s prompt.

Learned

What it taught me

The project taught me how to integrate an API into an existing platform.
