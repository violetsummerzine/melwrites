Moonbeam

Led global marketing in the Polkadot ecosystem for Moonbeam, an Ethereum-compatible smart contract platform.

Role
Global Marketing Lead
Focus
Web3 · Smart contracts · Polkadot
Years
2025
Audiences
Crypto investors, developers
Work

What I did

I was responsible for launching 1–2 campaigns per month with cross-functional input from engineering, research, community, and partnerships. I managed all campaign positioning, messaging, and audience development.

At Moonbeam, I launched “Moonrise Points,” a custom airdrop platform designed for the $GLMR token economy. The launch campaign included four partners, five-figure paid advertising campaigns across Web3 websites, and daily check-ins with 2,500 traders, all invested in winning 50,000 $GLMR. I managed the entire campaign workflow, from content creation to producing virtual meetings with paid influencer-led communities. The six-week campaign launch culminated in an increase in on-chain token transactions, a boost in blockchain wallets, an increase in social media followers on X, and an increase in the token price, which had previously stagnated.

Context

What Moonbeam is

Moonbeam is an Ethereum-compatible smart contract platform in the Polkadot ecosystem, built so developers can deploy Solidity contracts and use familiar Ethereum tools while reaching Polkadot’s cross-chain network.

Learned

What it taught me

What did working on a multichain smart contract platform teach me about developers, ecosystems, and infrastructure? Marketing can be gamified to boost engagement and ultimately drive growth in on-chain token holders, which directly benefits Moonbeam. Each GTM included some element of gamified tactics, as well as general technical acumen when it comes to downloading wallets and transferring assets from centralized exchanges like Crypto.com and Binance.
