About Melissa

Product-minded technologist

I make the products come alive and give them a fanbase — whether that’s working with developers or consumers in the tech, fashion, beauty, and entertainment spaces — and understand their behavior with data and revenue-driven metrics.

I have a B.A. in Political Communications from The George Washington University and have spent the last year building the groundwork of mainstream and independent digital media. As a writer and editor, I’ve managed content teams at AOL.com, The Huffington Post, and Gawker Media, and have also worked on the brand marketing side, leading deal flow for advertising campaigns, optimizing reach with content creation, as well as consulting many founders on GTM.

As a product strategist, writer, and technologist, my mission is to document the spirit and personality of culture using the latest and greatest emerging tech. I believe this can change the way people build, work, and make decisions for the better.

Over the last several years, that curiosity has led me to work for various venture-backed startups in the AI, fintech, and blockchain spaces. So far, I’ve built two blockchain products and worked alongside dozens of developers and technical communities, like women-led DAOs and NFT projects (Rug Radio, World of Women, The Clinic NFT, etc.). This has allowed me to explore everything from decentralized infrastructure and open payments to AI and human-generated training data.

Most recently, I completed a 10-month Data Science & Machine Learning certification, where I developed a stronger foundation in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, and data analysis — which sparked my work on regression model research for model behaviors.
