About Melissa

Product-minded technologist

I bring products to life and build their fan bases, working with developers and consumers across tech, fashion, beauty, and entertainment, and I measure what drives their behavior with data and revenue-focused metrics.

I have a B.A. in Political Communications from The George Washington University and have spent years helping build the foundations of mainstream and independent digital media. As a writer and editor, I managed content teams at AOL.com, The Huffington Post, and Gawker Media. On the brand marketing side, I led deal flow for advertising campaigns, expanded reach through content creation, and advised founders on go-to-market strategy.

As a product strategist, writer, and technologist, my mission is to capture the spirit and personality of culture through emerging technology. I believe this work can change how people build, work, and make decisions for the better.

Over the last several years, that curiosity has led me to venture-backed startups in AI, fintech, and blockchain. I’ve built two blockchain products and worked alongside dozens of developers and technical communities, including women-led DAOs and NFT projects such as Rug Radio, World of Women, and The Clinic NFT. That work has spanned everything from decentralized infrastructure and open payments to AI and human-generated training data.

I’m currently completing a 10-month Data Science & Machine Learning certification, where I’m developing a stronger foundation in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, and data analysis. The program sparked my current research using regression models to study model behavior.
