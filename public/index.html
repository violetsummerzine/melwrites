Statement

Mel Writes is the independent practice of Melissa, a writer and editor working across brand voice, editorial, and long-form storytelling. Projects range from campaign concepts and scripts to websites, reports, and audio.

I’m interested in writing in its broadest sense — from a single line of microcopy to a fifty-page narrative. My favorite projects are the ones that serve real readers and show resourcefulness; I hope they leave people seeing familiar things in new ways.

Good writing connects people to one another, to an idea, and to themselves.
