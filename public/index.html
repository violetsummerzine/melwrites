About Melissa

Product-minded technologist

I build at the intersection of product, data, and technology.

I’m Melissa Henderson — a product strategist, writer, and technologist interested in how data, AI, and emerging technologies change the way people build, work, and make decisions.

For more than a decade, I’ve worked across media, technology, Web3, marketing, and community — building products, launching technical projects, developing audiences, and translating complicated ideas into things people can actually understand and use.

Over the last several years, that curiosity has taken me deeper into the technical side of technology. I’ve built two blockchain products, worked alongside developers and technical communities, and explored everything from decentralized infrastructure and open payments to AI and human-generated training data.

Most recently, I completed a 10-month Data Science & Machine Learning program, where I developed a stronger foundation in Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, and data analysis — and discovered that I genuinely enjoy getting into the weeds.

  • Regression models.
  • Feature selection.
  • Model evaluation.
  • Data visualization.
  • Asking why the numbers look the way they do.

I’m particularly interested in product management, AI, data infrastructure, financial technology, and the systems that sit underneath emerging technologies. I like being close to both the problem and the data: understanding what we’re building, why we’re building it, what the numbers are telling us, and what we should do next.

My background in media and marketing gives me another layer: I know how to research, communicate, ask better questions, understand audiences, and turn complex technical ideas into compelling narratives. I’m interested in the space where product thinking meets technical depth, analytical rigor, and human behavior.
